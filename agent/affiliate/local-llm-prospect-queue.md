# Private AI partner prospect queue

Research date: 2026-07-16. This is a review queue, not an authorization to send
bulk messages. Re-open the cited work, identify a named contact and a public contact
route, then personalize the partner-kit message around one specific useful point.

## First review group

1. **KeepItTechie / KitPro** — published a practical Linux-focused walkthrough on
   running local AI without Big Tech. Strong audience match for the guide's
   beginner-safe privacy verification and cross-platform troubleshooting.
   Source: https://blog.kitpro.us/blog/local-ai-on-linux-run-your-own-ai-without-big-tech

2. **InsiderLLM** — publishes specifically about local-AI privacy. Review its
   current guide and editorial/contact policy before approaching; lead with the
   guide's distinction between a local model and a fully local data path.
   Source: https://insiderllm.com/guides/local-ai-privacy-guide/

3. **Breachfolio** — covers local LLM use for security notes, a narrow high-intent
   use case. A review copy may be relevant, but the message must preserve the
   product's disclaimer that it is not a compliance guarantee.
   Source: https://breachfolio.com/ai/run-a-local-llm-for-security-notes/

## Qualification gate

Contact only when all are true:

- Their work is still public and active.
- They accept product/resource pitches or provide a business contact route.
- A named article/video makes the message genuinely personal.
- They receive a review copy with no posting requirement.
- A 50% affiliate relationship is created only after interest is confirmed.
- Any published recommendation clearly discloses the commission.

Log outreach date, recipient, referenced work, response, affiliate link, clicks,
and sales. Stop after one invitation and one polite follow-up; do not scrape or
bulk-send.
