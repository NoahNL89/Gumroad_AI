**Body (paste, then trim to your voice):**
Most Instagram advice is either vague ("post consistently!") or trying to sell you a course. Here's the actual system I use, free — it's built around what the 2026 algorithm actually weights, not vanity likes:

1. **Optimize for saves, not likes.** Saves are the highest-weight engagement signal Instagram uses now. So I design posts people *bookmark for later*: checklists, step-by-step guides, resource lists, swipe files. Every caption ends with a save prompt, not just "double tap."
2. **Use a proven caption skeleton instead of freestyling.** My highest-save format: Hook (1 line) → Problem statement (2–3 lines) → Your insight (4–5 lines) → One actionable tip (2–3 lines) → Save prompt + a question CTA. That structure alone roughly doubled my saves versus rambling captions.
3. **Build carousels that force the swipe.** The "mistakes" format works every time: Slide 1 = "X mistakes [your audience] makes", slides 2–9 = one mistake per slide (bold headline + 2 lines), slide 10 = summary + follow CTA. Completing a carousel is a strong reach signal.
4. **Kill yes/no questions.** Binary questions get ignored. Multi-answer prompts get comments: "What's your experience with X?", "Which of these applies to you?", "What am I missing?"
5. **"Tag someone who needs this" is dead.** Content gets shared when it makes the *sharer* look smart or relatable to their own audience. "This explains everything" and "so relatable 😭" reactions drive far more organic shares in 2026.
